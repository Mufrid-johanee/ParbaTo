## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, immersive EdTech SaaS aesthetic that bridges institutional authority with gamified cognitive engagement. Tailored for forward-thinking educators, academic administrators, and ambitious self-directed learners, the visual experience avoids playful cartoonishness in favor of a sophisticated, scientific command-center atmosphere.

The style converges **Modern SaaS Precision** with **Controlled Glassmorphic Layering**:
- Deep, atmospheric slate and indigo voids replace sterile flat backgrounds to minimize visual fatigue during prolonged learning sprints.
- Luminous telemetry cues (electric indigo, cyan, emerald, and energetic coral) denote spatial presence in the digital twin, quest progression tiers, and real-time cognitive state indicators.
- Surfaces leverage delicate hairline borders, ambient violet back-glows, and high-legibility typographic hierarchies to deliver an engineering-grade, future-ready learning architecture.

## Colors

The system uses a dark foundation engineered specifically for data-dense telemetry dashboards, 3D classroom representations, and high-focus quest interfaces:

- **Primary (`#6366F1` / Electric Indigo):** Signals primary flow activations, primary focus rings, elevated spatial markers, and forward system progression.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` / Cyber Cyan):** Governs digital twin hardware states, synchronized node connections, live classroom sensors, and telemetry analytics.
- **Tertiary (`#EC4899` / Kinetic Coral):** Highlights high-priority quest objectives, achievement milestones, urgent time-bounds, and adaptive mastery rewards.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - **Success / Equilibrium (`#10B981`):** Competency validation, flex-path completion, and nominal sensor telemetry.
  - **Attention / Caution (`#F59E0B`):** Impending quest deadlines, adaptive difficulty warnings, and bandwidth fallbacks.
- **Neutral Palette:** Anchored around `#0B0F19` (Obsidian Base), scaling through `#111827` (Card / Section Surface), `#1F293D` (Elevated Panel), and `#374151` (Border Contrast), up to `#F9FAFB` for optical-grade primary text contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy coordinates three purpose-driven typefaces:
- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean geometry, confident terminal curves, and crisp legibility for dashboards, curriculum nodes, and dynamic quest headings.
- **Body (`Inter`):** Handles all long-form academic text, real-time transcripts, adaptive explanations, and system configurations with neutral optical balance.
- **Data & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical coordinates, spatial positioning values, learning timestamps, node IDs, and real-time cognitive metrics.

Headings at or above 32px automatically compress down to dedicated mobile scales on viewports beneath 768px to ensure structural containment without line-wrapping breaks.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform follows an adaptive 12-column responsive layout built primarily for data-dense multi-pane desktop workstations:
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with standard 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Accommodates dual-pane or tri-pane orchestrations: navigation rail (64px collapsed / 240px expanded), central interactive canvas (twin stage or quest tree), and contextual telemetry sidebar (360px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters. The auxiliary telemetry panel folds into a slide-over modal drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) screen margins. Complex twin views switch from multi-viewport rendering to single-viewport tabbed stacks.

Spacing intervals adhere to an 8pt architectural rhythm, with `space-xs` (4px) reserved exclusively for micro-alignments, tag internal spacing, and coordinate offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent planar layering, hairline luminance boundaries, and tinted ambient glows rather than physical skeuomorphic drop shadows:

- **Base Floor (`Level 0`):** Solid `#0B0F19` void canvas. Zero shadow.
- **Surface Level 1 (`Cards & Sidebars`):** `#111827` mixed with a 1px solid hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`. Subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (`Active Modules & Modals`):** Semi-transparent `#161F30` layered with a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a top edge highlight of `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)` to simulate overhead luminescent refraction.
- **Surface Level 3 (`Floating Overlays & Tooltips`):** `#1E293B` surrounded by an electric indigo halo: `0 10px 30px -5px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to balance modern enterprise authority with fluid, high-engagement ergonomics:
- **Base Components (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to standard interactive buttons, form input fields, segmented switches, and small telemetry cards.
- **Structural Containers (1rem / 16px):** Applied to learning modules, dynamic classroom feeds, video stream frames, and modal windows.
- **Hero & Interactive Canvases (1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for quest pathway clusters, 3D viewport viewports, and primary platform dashboards.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic status tags, learner level badges, live audio indicators, and micro-avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Gradient fill `linear-gradient(135deg, #6366F1 0%, #4F46E5 100%)`, crisp white text, 8px corner radius, hairline inset top-border `rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`. On hover: translates -1px with ambient glow `0 6px 20px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary (Cyan Telemetry):** Transparent background, 1px border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`, text `#06B6D4`. On hover: `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)` wash.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, text `#9CA3AF`, hover to `#F9FAFB` with background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Pill-shaped with a live pulsing 6px dot indicator. Background tinted at 15% opacity matching the semantic state (e.g., `#10B981` at 15% for online twin nodes). Text set in `JetBrains Mono` at 11px uppercase.
- **Quest Mission Tags:** Chamfered or 6px rounded, featuring tertiary coral accents (`#EC4899`) with subtle XP icons.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Dark slate background `#111827`, hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, 8px roundedness. On focus: border transitions to `#6366F1` with an indigo micro-ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`. Monospace helper text positioned in the upper right corner.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom 18px squares/circles with `#1F293D` base and 1.5px border. Checked state snaps with an animated scale into `#6366F1` featuring a crisp white geometric icon.

### Cards & Mission Panels
- **Standard Card:** `#111827` background, 1px hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, 16px corner radius, internal padding of 24px (`space-lg`).
- **Interactive Twin & Quest Node Card:** Adds an ambient hover state with a gradient border shift from neutral to `#6366F1` and a smooth upward lift of 2px.

### Specialized EdTech Components
- **Digital Twin Sync Visualizer:** Viewport with a subtle spatial grid overlay (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.05)` at 20px subdivisions) displaying live student spatial distribution, connected audio feeds, and active sensor pins.
- **FlexLearn Adaptive Skill Tree:** Node-link network diagrams connected via SVG bezier splines. Mastery nodes reflect glowing states (unlocked nodes pulse cyan `#06B6D4`, completed paths light in emerald `#10B981`, and target quests highlight in coral `#EC4899`).
